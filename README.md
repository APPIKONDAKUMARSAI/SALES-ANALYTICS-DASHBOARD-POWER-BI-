# SALES-ANALYTICS-DASHBOARD-POWER-BI-

## Overview

This project analyzes sales transactions from Jan 2021 to Dec 2021 (extended into early 2022) to uncover trends across regions, products, and salespersons.

The goal is to convert raw sales data into actionable business insights using Power BI.

---

## Key Metrics

* **Total Sales Amount:** ₹17,82,570
* **Total Transactions:** 366
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 1607
* **Total Products:** 6

---

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Overview

* KPI cards for quick performance tracking
* Region filters (East, West, North, South)
* Sales distribution across regions

### 2. Product Analysis

* Total item sales by category
* Revenue contribution by product
* Identification of high-value vs high-volume products

### 3. Salesperson Analysis

* Total sales by salesperson
* Monthly performance trends
* Contribution comparison

### 4. Trend Analysis

* Monthly sales variations
* Identification of peak and low-performing periods

---

## Key Insights

* **High-value products drive revenue**
  Example: Printers generate more revenue despite lower quantity

* **Top contributors dominate sales**
  Example: Siddhu and Rohit Das contribute a large share of total sales

* **Regional imbalance exists**
  Example: East region shows highest activity and consistency

* **Volume ≠ Revenue**
  Example: Keyboards sell frequently but contribute less revenue
